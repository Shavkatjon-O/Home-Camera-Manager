The TV app in apps/tv shows a grid of every camera on an Android TV. It connects straight to the cameras on the local Wi-Fi, decodes them on the TV and needs nothing from the server. [Getting started](../getting-started/tv.mdx) covers building and installing it.

## Using it

- Grid: the D-pad moves a thin outline between tiles and briefly shows the camera name and lens; OK opens that tile fullscreen. A tile without a picture shows a quiet status: Connecting, Reconnecting in N s, Offline, Decoder limit or the login error.
- Fullscreen: Back returns to the grid. The camera's sound plays only here, and the mute and volume keys work as usual. On a PTZ camera, holding an arrow pans or tilts the camera until the key is released; on other cameras left and right step to the previous or next tile.
- Menu, or a long press on OK, opens Settings, Diagnostics and Reload.
- Settings: OK on an address opens the TV keyboard, and a confirmed address applies at once. A switch turns a camera off. Find V380 cameras searches the Wi-Fi and moves a V380 camera to the address that answers with its device id. Reset to defaults returns to the compiled values.
- Diagnostics: per camera the state, stream, resolution, frame rate and bit rate, decoder, decode delay, time to the first picture, audio format and last error; then the device, the app's memory, the decoders in use and every HEVC and AVC decoder with its instance limit and maximum size, and the events log.

## Cameras it plays

| Camera | Protocol | Grid | Fullscreen |
|---|---|---|---|
| EZVIZ and other RTSP cameras | RTSP over TCP with digest or basic login | sub stream | main stream with sound |
| V380 | the V380 LAN protocol on port 8800 | small stream | large stream with sound |

A V380 picture holds both lenses stacked, lens 1 on top and the moving lens 2 below, and the app shows each as its own tile.

## How it plays them

- One session and one decoder per camera, never per tile. The grid runs every enabled camera on its small stream; fullscreen stops the grid sessions and opens only that camera's large stream with sound, and Back reverses it.
- Decoding uses MediaCodec in asynchronous mode and renders each frame as soon as it is decoded, hardware decoders first. A camera whose decoder cannot start shows Decoder limit and retries every 30 s, and a fullscreen stream larger than the hardware decoder allows falls back to the small stream.
- Every decoder draws into its own texture, and one OpenGL ES 2.0 surface composes the visible tiles, each letterboxed to keep its aspect.
- Each camera reconnects on its own after 1, 2, 5, 10 and then every 30 s. A V380 camera that does not answer at start triggers one discovery, and losing and regaining the network reconnects everything.
