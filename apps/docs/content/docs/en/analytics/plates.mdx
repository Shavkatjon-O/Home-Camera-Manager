On views with plates turned on, the engine reads number plates on vehicle events:

1. It cuts the vehicle a few times from the recording buffer at full resolution, because the 1080p detector frame shrinks distant plates.
2. A text detector, PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv4 DB detector, finds plate-shaped lines of text; NVIDIA LPDNet is an optional finder under its own licence (SSS_ANALYTICS_PLATE_FINDER).
3. Each line is levelled into a straight band, because a plate seen from above leans, and read by the PP-OCRv4 English recogniser.
4. The text is fitted to the Uzbek formats, such as 01 A 123 BC and 01 123 ABC, swapping look-alike letters and digits only where the format needs the other kind, and the reads of an event vote.

Plates are searchable on the Search page and shown as candidates on the event.

## Limits

- Daylight only: infrared light makes plates glare white at night, so nearly colourless pictures are refused instead of storing garbage.
- The region code is rarely read, and distant cars are too small.
