## Search

The Search page, and the Search tab of the mobile app, filter every event by time, cameras, views, kinds, labels, zones, lines, person or cluster and alerts, as a list or a grid. Find similar ranks faces like a chosen face or an uploaded photo.

## Find this person and journeys

<Status kind="beta" />

Find this person, on any person event, finds the same person on other cameras by appearance, and a journey links a person's tracks across cameras, such as yard, then entrance, then corridor.

- The engine embeds person boxes about once a second with OSNet-AIN. Raw similarity between any two people is about 0.5 on typical cameras, so tracks are compared after taking away the running mean of all tracks; unrelated tracks then score near 0.
- A finished track links to the mutual best match of another view that ended within 3 minutes above 0.35, and nothing links before 20 tracks have been seen.
- Daylight colour and infrared night pictures of the same person do not match, so journeys and Find this person work within the same light only.

Appearances and journeys are deleted with their events, so Find this person never offers a sighting whose event is gone.
